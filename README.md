> **Close The Gap.**

**The Gap** is the dangerous distance between what was said and what was actually understood. In operational systems, the full chain is:

```
SAID → HEARD → UNDERSTOOD → ACTED → VERIFIED
```

High-Consequence Communication treats communication as an operational control rather than a soft skill. Its purpose is to reduce ambiguity, expose hidden misunderstanding, protect the right to interrupt, and preserve human judgment when pressure, hierarchy, automation, and incomplete information distort meaning.

## Core Question

> **Did meaning survive the handoff?**

The doctrine applies wherever misunderstanding can change a mission outcome: aviation, maritime autonomy, defense, healthcare, emergency response, manufacturing, and human-machine operations.

## Doctrine

The repository develops a practical system around:

- **The Gap** — the distance between transmitted meaning and operational understanding.
- **Cold Phase / Hot Phase** — separate the conditions for deliberation from the conditions for execution.
- **S-E-R** — Stop. Establish. Resolve.
- **Readback Contract** — critical communication closes the loop.
- **Communication Triage** — distinguish vocabulary gaps, shortcuts, and secrets.
- **Silence Is Data** — missing or non-standard responses are signals, not empty space.
- **Truth Velocity** — how quickly consequential bad news reaches someone able to act.
- **The Veto Doctrine** — preserve human authority to stop unsafe or incoherent machine action.
- **The Final Sensor** — the human who challenges the map when the terrain disagrees.

See [The Gap](doctrine/the-gap.md) and [Doctrine Map](doctrine/doctrine-map.md).
