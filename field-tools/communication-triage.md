# Communication Triage

When communication breaks, diagnose the failure before choosing the correction.

## 1. Vocabulary Gap
The receiver does not know the word or term.

**Response:** define, rephrase, standardize.

## 2. Shortcut
The receiver knows the language but slang, abbreviation, or compression creates ambiguity.

**Response:** restore standard phraseology and full callouts.

## 3. Secret
The receiver is confused but social pressure, hierarchy, embarrassment, or time pressure suppresses clarification.

**Response:** stop the sequence and force clarification.

The third category is particularly dangerous because it can remain invisible until action exposes it.

**Confusion is data.**
