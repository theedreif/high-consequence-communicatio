# Assumption Declaration

When action must continue with incomplete information, make the assumption visible.

Use the pattern:

> **ASSUMING** [condition], then [current conclusion].  
> **IF** that assumption is wrong, we expect [observable indicator].  
> **IF** that indicator appears, we [predefined action].

This converts a hidden assumption into a testable proposition and gives the team an explicit trigger for reconsideration.

Do not substitute this tool for required safety, legal, regulatory, or operational procedures.
