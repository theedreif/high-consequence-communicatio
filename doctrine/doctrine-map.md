# Doctrine Map

High-Consequence Communication is organized around five layers.

## 1. Philosophy
- The Gap
- Map is not territory
- Trust physics over pixels
- Clarity as survival
- Silence as data

## 2. Protocols
- S-E-R: Stop → Establish → Resolve
- Readback Contract
- Phrase discipline
- Assumption declaration
- Forced specificity

## 3. Training
- Stress inoculation
- Retrieval under pressure
- Scenario variation
- Error-based learning
- Demonstrated behavior over completion

## 4. Culture
- Psychological safety for clarification
- Just Culture
- Forced dissent for consequential decisions
- Truth Velocity

## 5. Operator
- Cognitive sovereignty
- Final Sensor
- Veto Doctrine
- Human judgment at the automation boundary

The architecture is designed to answer one question:

> **Did meaning survive the handoff?**
