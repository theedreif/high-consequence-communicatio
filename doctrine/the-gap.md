# The Gap

**The Gap** is the distance between what was said and what was understood.

In a high-consequence system, that distance extends across the whole operational chain:

```
SAID → HEARD → UNDERSTOOD → ACTED → VERIFIED
```

A communication is not complete because words were transmitted. It is complete when the intended meaning survives the handoff, produces the intended action, and the result is verified.

## Why The Gap Matters

Pressure compresses attention. Hierarchy suppresses challenge. Fluency can imitate understanding. Automation can execute a misunderstanding quickly and consistently. Silence can conceal uncertainty.

The doctrine therefore treats communication as observable operational behavior.

## Design Rule

For every critical instruction ask:

1. What exactly must be understood?
2. What readback demonstrates that understanding?
3. What observable action should follow?
4. What confirms the intended result occurred?
5. What interruption is available when any link breaks?

**Clarity is not a style choice. It is an operational control.**
